# Tech Compassion（科技仁心）

A harmonious fusion of four themes — combining the technological boldness of Tech Innovation, the professional depth of Ocean Depths, the clean readability of Modern Minimalist, and the crisp clarity of Arctic Frost.

最适合助残公益科技类路演的全新融合主题。

## Color Palette

- **Electric Blue**: `#0066ff` - Primary accent (from Tech Innovation)
- **Deep Navy**: `#1a2332` - Deep backgrounds (from Ocean Depths)
- **Charcoal**: `#36454f` - Body text for readability (from Modern Minimalist)
- **Steel Blue**: `#4a6fa5` - Secondary accent (from Arctic Frost)
- **Ice Blue**: `#d4e4f7` - Light card backgrounds (from Arctic Frost)
- **Teal**: `#2d8b8b` - Success highlights (from Ocean Depths)
- **Warm White**: `#fafafa` - Page backgrounds (from Arctic Frost)
- **Pure White**: `#ffffff` - Text on dark backgrounds
